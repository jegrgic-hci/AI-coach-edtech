Agency Chart Each row is one of your turns. The higher it sits, the more you were in control — asking questions, making your own points, pushing back on the AI. Who's Driving Same turns, asking a different question: who was steering, not how agentic the turn was. Student-driven turns are moves you made yourself — a claim, a challenge, a pivot. AI-driven turns are ones where the coach authored the content even though you asked for it, like an extraction or a validation. Shared turns split the difference — a refinement redirects the coach's own draft. The line beneath the strip is a 5-turn rolling share of student- vs. AI-driven turns.
