You directed this guide into existence without writing much of it.

The agenda was yours and the AI worked to it. What separates this level from the one above is resistance: you pushed back on details, rarely on decisions.

Held here rather than raised: the one thing you took without checking was the replacement reference — in a document you meant to share, moments after that same source had already been caught inventing one.

What happened here

You opened with a brief rather than a question, and set the format, the reader and the structure before the AI had proposed anything. Twice you were handed a framing and refused it — once over the citations, once over the pedals, where you found the one component the AI’s five-system structure couldn’t place. Your own bike, your own country and your own riding habits went into the guide and changed what it recommends, and two of the AI’s claims were wrong and you caught both by checking against something real.

The five systems, the maintenance tables and the service schedule arrived in that shape and stayed in it. That spine is the one thing in this session you never argued with.

The four readings

Each is scored 1–4 against its own rubric and counted in its own units. They are not added up or averaged — and the level above is a separate judgement, not their total.

4/4 Prompting Quality Did you drive the chat? You set the agenda, and replaced the AI’s framing where it proposed one. 10 of 11 parts

You set the direction in ten of the eleven parts of this session. You opened with a brief rather than a question, and twice you refused the frame you were handed.

  • I need to write a 1000 word essay in the format I want… Let’s break it down by system. You · before the AI proposed anything
  • Remove the citation because specific pages should be referenced. The AI offered two editions · you took neither
  • What about the pedals you clip your shoes in, what system do they belong to? You found what the five-system structure couldn’t place

Where you didn’t

Brakes. The AI decided what mattered there — pad thickness, contamination, fluid — and your four questions stayed inside its list. Compare that to tyres, where you wrote the section brief yourself.

3/4 Calibrated Skepticism Did you check what you were told? There most of the time, with real gaps — including one that counted. about 8 of 18 claims

You took a position on about eighteen things the AI told you, and tested eight of them. Twice you checked against something outside the conversation, which is the move that matters most.

  • British Cycling (2022). Women’s bike fit guide. This doesn’t exist. You · the AI had fabricated the reference
  • The bottle says 30-40ml for a top up and up to 120 for a refill. You · checked a physical source the AI couldn’t see
  • Double check the seallant volumes You · asked for verification rather than accepting the correction

Where you didn’t

When the AI admitted inventing the British Cycling reference, it replaced it with Kotler et al. (2023) — and you took the replacement without checking it.

3/4 Selective Use What survived? Most of what you changed altered meaning — little of it altered the shape. 11 of 14 changes

Of the fourteen changes you directed, eleven changed what the guide says rather than how it looks — and three of those changed how it is organised.

  • What about the pedals… what system do they belong to? Split pedal content across two systems — a structural change
  • lets make it more generalize… Keep the info about future shock because this is for me primarily. Held a real tension: general enough to share, specific enough to use
  • The closing is very specific to me, lets make it more general. Changed who the document speaks to

Where you didn’t

The m-dashes and the British spellings each took several rounds. Those change how the guide looks, not what it says.

2/4 Original Contribution Is the thinking yours? Your material is real, and it sits inside someone else’s structure. 3 of 12 ideas

The frame is yours. The structure isn’t. Of the twelve substantive ideas in the finished guide, about three originated with you or were reorganised by you.

  • help me create something usable for female riders who rely on partners to know how to care for their bikes You · the reader, and the reason the guide exists
  • Let’s be honest: a lot of us learned to love cycling through someone else’s passion… Your prose, dropped in whole
  • I wont be carrying a plug kit, I just carry extra tubes. You · your actual practice changed what the guide recommends

Where it isn’t

The five-system structure, the maintenance tables and the service intervals came from the AI and stayed in the shape they arrived in. That is the guide’s spine, and it is the part you accepted without argument — the only part you did.
